# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour includes using welcoming and inclusive language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the project, and showing empathy toward others.

Unacceptable behaviour includes sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, harassment, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to `shivayogih72@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with privacy respected as far as practical.

Project maintainers may remove or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that conflict with this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for harmful behaviour.
